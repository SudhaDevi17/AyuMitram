## 1. Executive Summary & The "One-Liner"

> **"Ayu Mitram is the decentralized AI care intelligence layer that turns every grassroots visit to an adult aged 50+ into verified health, emotional, and financial outcomes—at zero cost to the elder."**

India does not lack frontline people or welfare entitlements; **it lacks the follow-through intelligence to connect the two.** Rather than spending tens of millions building an expensive, owned care workforce, Ayu Mitram plugs into existing community workforces (women’s Self-Help Groups, state volunteer time-banks, NGOs, and remote families) to convert informal conversations and prescription photos into verified government schemes, generic medicine savings, and clinical telemedicine appointments.

## 4. The Product Architecture & 5-Step Workflow

Ayu Mitram operates on a continuous, closed-loop 5-step operational engine:

```
[1. Enrol] ───> [2. Visit] ───> [3. Understand/Plan] ───> [4. Act] ───> [5. Follow-up]
Spoken Native   Voice Note +    Gemini Multimodal Scribe   Assigned to    3-Day SLA;
Audio Consent   Prescription    + Python Rules Engine:     Helper, Kin,   Verified Hours;
+ Trusted Kin   Snap (Vision)   Unlocks ₹ Schemes & Meds   or Proxy       Outcomes Audited
```

1. **Enrol (Zero-Screen Consent):** Elder provides native spoken consent (Telugu, Hindi, English). Tokenized ABHA ID is linked without collecting raw Aadhaar cards.
2. **Visit (Multimodal Ingestion):** Frontline helper speaks naturally for up to 2 minutes (*"Venkatamma is dizzy; BP tablets are finished"*), accompanied by a photo of the medicine strip. For non-verbal elders, pictographic check-in boards are used.
3. **Understand & Plan (Rules + LLM):** Gemini structures unstructured dialect speech into FHIR health entities. Deterministic Python rules match entitlements and generic medicine pricing.
4. **Act (Deterministic Task Delegation):** Actions become single-owner tasks:
   - **Age 50–59:** PMJJBY insurance audit, EPS-95 pension check, free NP-NCD health screening.
   - **Age 60–69:** State Old Age Pensions and central IGNOAPS welfare grants.
   - **Age 70+:** Universal **₹5,00,000 Ayushman Bharat Vaya Vandana** cashless cover.
   - **Clinical:** Jan Aushadhi generic substitution (saving ₹500–₹1,800/mo) and free eSanjeevani doctor booking.
5. **Follow-Up (3-Day Escalation SLA):** Tasks not fulfilled within 3 days automatically escalate: Primary Helper $\rightarrow$ Trusted Contact / Family $\rightarrow$ Programme Coordinator.

## 5. Blue Ocean Strategy & Competitive Moats

### 5.1 Four Actions Framework (ERRC)
* **Eliminate:** Owning a paid care workforce; requiring elders to own or use an app; one-off transactional fee barriers.
* **Reduce:** Helper training requirements (AI provides conversational prompts); cost-per-elder to marginal server costs; administrative reporting friction for NGOs.
* **Raise:** Health and financial outcomes delivered per visit; trust via multi-factor visit verification; remote family peace of mind.
* **Create:** A universal AI care layer that any community network can plug into; a portable, longitudinal care record across disparate NGOs (via ABDM); automated statutory scheme unlocking.

### 5.2 The 8-Moat Scorecard (Target: 25/40 in 3–5 Years)
1. **Workflow Embedding:** Once an SHG federation or state time-bank runs its daily volunteer hours and task routing through Ayu Mitram, switching costs are prohibitive.
2. **Channel Distribution Advantage:** Each partner network (K-DISC, Kudumbashree, SERP) acts as a zero-CAC customer acquisition channel.
3. **Cross-Network Outcome Data:** Proprietary data asset of real-world rural geriatric adherence, vernacular dialects, and frontline care interventions across India.
4. **Software Scale Economics:** Near-zero marginal cost per additional elder served.

## 8. Enterprise Governance, Elder Safety & Anti-Fraud

1. **Vetted Workforce Pipeline:** Zero open gig marketplaces. Helpers are exclusively onboarded via institutional partners with police clearances and local references.
2. **Zero-Cash & Zero-OTP Rule:** Helpers are strictly prohibited from touching physical currency, taking custody of original identity cards, or handling banking OTPs.
3. **Dual-Authorization Protocol for Destitute Elders:** For seniors living alone without family (`ALONE_NO_KIN`), scheme and financial applications require a dual cryptographic approval: Primary Helper + Panchayat Ward Member / NGO Supervisor.
4. **Multi-Factor Proof of Presence (MFPoP):** Visit hours are credited only when:
   $$\text{Verified} = (\text{GPS} \le 200m) \land (\text{Audio Liveness Sign-off} \lor \text{Ayu Card QR Scan})$$
5. **60-Second Instant Account Suspension:** Any reported concern or suspected financial extortion automatically freezes the helper's account network-wide within 60 seconds, triggering a mandatory 24-hour supervisor investigation.

## 9. Regulatory & Data Protection Compliance

* **DPDP Act (2023) Compliant:** 
  * Verifiable audio consent artifacts captured in local dialects before any data processing.
  * Right to withdraw consent instantly anonymizes/blinds elder PII.
  * Data Fiduciary vs. Data Processor roles explicitly delineated in institutional partner master service agreements (MSAs).
* **Zero-Aadhaar Persistence Policy (UIDAI Compliant):** No 12-digit Aadhaar numbers or raw biometrics are stored in databases. Automated client-side computer vision filters redact identity card scans before upload.
* **National Health Authority ABDM Standards:** Interoperable with the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (Milestones M1: ABHA creation; M2: HIP health record publishing; M3: HIU consented exchange via HL7 FHIR bundles).
* **Non-Diagnostic Clinical Guardrail:** Deterministic engine completely blocks Gemini from generating clinical diagnoses or modifying dosages, strictly routing observations to **eSanjeevani** or PHC medical officers.
